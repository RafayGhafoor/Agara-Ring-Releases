The first release of **AGARA Health** (formerly Agara Ring) for Android.

**Highlights**
- **Your day at a glance** — steps, sleep (night + naps), heart rate, blood oxygen and more on Today, with charts you can scrub for exact values.
- **Measure on demand** — heart rate, blood oxygen, blood pressure, stress, blood glucose and blood components. Measurements keep running if you leave the screen, with a notification when they finish in the background.
- **Automatic measuring** — choose how often the ring measures on its own, per metric.
- **Sleep** — stages, score and a night timeline; naps count towards your day.
- **Fast sync** — only new data is fetched on each refresh, with day-by-day progress.
- **Find your ring** — a live "getting warmer" circle from the ring's Bluetooth signal, plus make-it-vibrate.
- **Ring firmware** — check for updates from the app.
- **Appearance** — System / Dark / Light and four Agara colour themes.
- **Your data** — export and import compressed backups (`.json.gz`); nothing leaves your phone unless you choose.

**Install:** download `app-universal-release.apk` below and open it on your phone. Checksums: `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Estimates from the ring's optical sensor (blood pressure, glucose, blood components) are for wellness, not diagnosis.
