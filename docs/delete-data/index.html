How to delete your data or account

You choose what to delete. Your data on the phone and your Agara Cloud account are separate, so there are three ways, and you can use one or all.

1. Delete your Agara account and cloud data (in the app)

  1. Open AGARA Heart and go to Settings › Agara Cloud.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm, and enter your password again when asked.

This permanently erases every reading, daily total and sleep record Agara Cloud holds for you, the list of your devices and your account, and removes your sign-in record from Google Firebase Authentication. It cannot be undone. If Google's part cannot finish immediately, we retry automatically until it does. The data on your phone stays. Encrypted server backups made before the deletion are replaced as they rotate out, within 90 days.

Forgot your password but still have the app? Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen, then delete the account as above.

2. Delete your account without the app (for example, you lost your phone or cannot sign in)

  1. Email support@agaralife.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Agara account". Do not send a password.
  2. We reply to that address to confirm the request. We only act after you confirm from that address.
  3. We then erase your cloud data and account, and tell you when it is done, within 30 days of your confirmation.

This erases the same data as option 1. If you do not have access to that email address, we cannot verify that the account is yours and we will not delete it on request; reply to us with any other way you can show the account is yours, and we will tell you what we can do.

3. Delete the data on your phone

  1. Open AGARA Heart.
  2. Go to Settings › Privacy › Delete all my data.
  3. Confirm. This removes every reading, workout and setting stored by the app on that phone and cannot be undone.

If you are signed in to Agara Cloud, this also signs you out on that phone but does not delete your cloud account or cloud data. To delete those, use option 1 first. Uninstalling the app also removes its data from the phone.

Data in Apple Health or Health Connect

If you allowed AGARA Heart to write to Apple Health or Health Connect, that data stays there until you remove it in those apps: Health › Sharing › Apps › AGARA Heart on iPhone, or Health Connect › App permissions › AGARA Heart on Android.
