How to delete your data

The current Agara Ring app has no accounts and keeps your data on your phone, so you can delete it yourself in a minute.

In the app (iPhone, iPad and Android)

  1. Open Agara Ring.
  2. Go to Settings › Privacy › Delete all my data.
  3. Confirm. This removes every reading, workout and setting stored by the app on that phone. It cannot be undone.

Or uninstall the app

Uninstalling the app removes its stored data from the phone.

Data in Apple Health or Health Connect

If you allowed Agara Ring to write to Apple Health or Health Connect, that data stays there until you remove it in those apps: Health › Sharing › Apps › Agara Ring on iPhone, or Health Connect › App permissions › Agara Ring on Android.
