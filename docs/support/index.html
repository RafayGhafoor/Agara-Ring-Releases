Ring and app

My ring won't pair

  • Turn Bluetooth on and keep the ring within a metre of the phone.
  • Charge the ring, then place it on the charger and take it off again to wake it.
  • Make sure the ring isn't connected to another phone or tablet — a ring connects to one device at a time.
  • On Android, allow "Nearby devices"; on iPhone/iPad, allow Bluetooth for AGARA Heart.

My data looks incomplete

Open the app and pull down to sync. The ring keeps about three days of history, so sync at least every few days.

Are the readings medical-grade?

No. AGARA Heart is a wellness product. Readings are estimates, not medical advice.

Where do I download the Android app?

Find it on Google Play, or download signed builds from the GitHub releases page.

Agara account and Agara Cloud

An account is optional and only needed if you want to back up or sync your data. Find it in Settings › Agara Cloud.

I didn't get the verification email

  • Check the address shown on the verification screen is the one you meant, and look in your spam or junk folder.
  • Wait a minute, then tap Resend (there is a short wait between sends).
  • Tap I've verified after you open the link in the email. Open the link on any device.
  • You have 72 hours after creating the account to verify. After that syncing pauses until you verify; your data on the phone is not affected.
  • Still nothing? Email support@agaralife.com from the same address.

I forgot my password or it says my password is wrong

  • Tap Forgot password on the sign-in screen and we will email you a reset link. Check spam too.
  • For your security, the sign-in and sign-up messages do not say whether an email address has an account.
  • After too many wrong tries sign-in is paused for a while; wait and try again.

Sync is not working

  • Check you are signed in and your email is verified (the screen shows it), and that the phone is online.
  • Tap Sync now. Background sync runs about every two hours when your phone allows it, and when you open the app.
  • Sync pauses while sample data is shown. Leave sample mode to resume.
  • If the app says to update, install the latest version.
  • If it says your account was deleted, you have been signed out; your data on the phone is untouched.
  • Still stuck? Send us the app version and the message on screen.

How do I delete my account or data?

In the app: Settings › Agara Cloud › Delete account. If you cannot sign in, or for any other way, see How to delete your data or account.
